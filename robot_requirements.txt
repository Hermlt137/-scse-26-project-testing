1. The robot must determine the direction to move based on the availability of unblocked paths and the location of its goal.  
2. The robot must prioritize moving toward its goal whenever this can be done safely.  
3. The robot must never move into a direction that is blocked.  
4. If there is no safe direction available, the robot must stop.  
5. The robot must receive input indicating whether the path in front, to the left, and to the right is blocked.  
6. The robot must receive input indicating whether its goal is ahead, to the left, or to the right.  
7. The navigation logic must be capable of operating in multiple simulated environments.
